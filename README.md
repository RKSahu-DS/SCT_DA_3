# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Dashboard | SkillCraft Technology Task 03

## 📝 Overview
This repository contains an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to answer a critical business question: **"Why are employees leaving?"** 

Developed as Task 03 for the Data Analytics virtual internship at SkillCraft Technology, this report analyzes the HR Employee Attrition dataset to uncover the primary factors driving employee turnover.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI:** Data visualization, interactive dashboard creation, and Key Influencer analysis.
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query for data cleaning and formatting.

## 🔍 Key Features
* **Interactive Filtering:** Users can dynamically slice the data by **Region**, **Age Group**, and **Department** to see localized attrition trends.
* **Key Influencers Analysis:** Utilizes AI-driven visuals to determine the exact likelihood of attrition based on specific employee attributes.
* **Comprehensive Metrics:** Tracks Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, and Average Monthly Income.

## 💡 Key Insights Discovered
Based on the dashboard analysis, the highest risk factors for employee attrition are:
1. **Overtime:** Employees working overtime have a 30.5% attrition rate compared to 10.4% for those who don't.
2. **Career Stage & Age:** Employees under 25 experience a 39.2% attrition rate, and those in their first year are 2.7x more likely to leave.
3. **Compensation:** Leavers earned an average of $4,787/month, significantly less than the $6,833/month average of those who stayed.
4. **Travel:** Frequent travelers exhibit a 24.9% attrition rate versus just 8.0% for non-travelers.
5. **Stock Options:** Employees with zero stock options face a 24.4% attrition rate.
